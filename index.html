About Us - KhabarSetu

KhabarSetu ek hyperlocal news website hai jo Jamwa Ramgarh, Jaipur aur Rajasthan ki taza khabron ko ek jagah par laati hai.

Hamara maksad hai gaon, kasba aur shahar ki har chhoti-badi khabar ko aasaan bhasha (Hindi) me aap tak pahunchana. Hum koshish karte hain ki khabar bina milawat ke, seedhi aur sachchi ho.

Ham kya cover karte hain?
- Rajasthan / Jaipur ki breaking news
- Education (RBSE, College, Jobs)
- Mausam, Kheti, Gaon ki khabrein
- Sarkari Yojana aur local events

KhabarSetu ki shuruaat 2026 me Jaipur se hui thi. Hum student aur yuva team hain jo digital patrakarita ko gaon tak le jana chahte hain.
